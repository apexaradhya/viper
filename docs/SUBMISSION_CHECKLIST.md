# Congressional App Challenge 2026: submission checklist

Source: official 2026 rules (congressionalappchallenge.us/students/rules and the 2026 rules PDF). Re-read the PDF once yourself before you submit; the portal is the final authority.

**DEADLINE: Monday, October 26, 2026, 12:00 pm EDT. Registration and submission must both be done by then. Submissions cannot be changed afterward.**
**Your target: submit by Sunday, Oct 25, 8:00 pm. Monday morning is a buffer only (and you have school).**

## A. Do tonight, before coding more (registration can take time)
- [ ] Go to congressionalappchallenge.us -> Student Registration.
- [ ] Have ready: a **personal email (not your school email)**, **home address with 9-digit ZIP**, **school address with 9-digit ZIP**, your **Congressional District and Member of Congress** for both, and a **parent or guardian's name and email**. A coding teacher or mentor name/email is optional.
- [ ] Look up your district at house.gov/representatives/find-your-representative. The participating-districts page lists PA06 (Chrissy Houlahan) as participating as of Oct 23, 2026. Confirm that is your home or school district. You can compete in your home district or your school district, but only one.
- [ ] Complete the personal profile and eligibility quiz. If eligible you get an email that lets you start the application. Check your inbox and spam.
- [ ] Individual entry is fine. Teams max four; you do not need one.

## B. Eligibility facts to confirm
- [ ] Enrolled in middle or high school on Oct 26, 2026. (Yes.)
- [ ] U.S. resident at time of submission.
- [ ] App created **after October 30, 2025**. (S.N.A.K.E. software starts now, so fine.)
- [ ] **One app per person per year.** Do not enter anything else.

## C. App requirements
- [ ] Demonstrates some degree of functionality (the full flow runs on the synthetic sample).
- [ ] Original and created by you. Open-source libraries are allowed but **must be clearly documented** (done in README and `docs/AI_USE_DISCLOSURE.md`: Streamlit, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-image, OpenCV, Pillow).
- [ ] **AI use fully disclosed**, supporting only specific parts, not the entire technical development. You must show significant individual contribution and technical understanding. (Your STUDENT functions, your tests passing, your AI log.)
- [ ] No third-party rights: no copyrighted images/data without a license. Credit any dataset in `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`.
- [ ] Content suitable: no inappropriate content; no violation of privacy rights. No real patient images.
- [ ] Judges may request your app and source code to verify it works as described. **Failure to honor that request disqualifies the entry.** Put the code on GitHub (public repo, or be ready to share) and make sure the app runs from the README instructions.

## D. Demonstration video (the most critical component)
- [ ] 1 to 3 minutes. Over-length videos may be penalized.
- [ ] Uploaded to **YouTube or Vimeo** and set to **Public**.
- [ ] It must include: **the name of each participant**, **the name of the app**, **the purpose in one clear sentence**, **the target audience**, **the tools and coding languages used**, and a **demonstration of the functionality**.
- [ ] Script is in `docs/DEMO_VIDEO_SCRIPT.md`.
- [ ] Check the video link works in a private/incognito window.

## E. Written questions (answers drafted in `docs/SUBMISSION_ANSWERS.md`)
1. Title of your app
2. Explain the app's purpose
3. What inspired you to create this app?
4. What technical/coding difficulty did you face, and how did you address it?
5. What did you learn while participating? Biggest takeaway?
6. What would you change in a 2.0 version?
(The portal may add or reword questions. Read each one before pasting.)

## F. Final pass (Sunday evening)
- [ ] `pytest -q` shows all tests passing, no skips.
- [ ] Fresh-clone test: clone the repo into a new folder, follow the README, app runs.
- [ ] GitHub repo URL works logged out.
- [ ] Video URL works logged out.
- [ ] Every answer in the form is final (no edits after the deadline).
- [ ] Submit. Save the confirmation email or screenshot.

## G. After the deadline
- [ ] Complete the **Exit Questionnaire** link you receive (teams: each member individually).
- [ ] Results are embargoed until January 15, 2027; judging happens in November, winners are announced by December per the site.
- [ ] Winners are featured on House.gov and the contest site; entering grants Congress and the administrator a license to post your submission online for noncommercial use, while you keep ownership.
